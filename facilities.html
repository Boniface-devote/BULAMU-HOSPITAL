Facilities & equipment

Built for modern medicine.

We invest where it counts — diagnostics, theatres and monitoring — so your doctor decides with the full picture.

Inside our walls, every machine earns its place. Our key equipment includes:

  • ECG & cardiac monitoring12-lead electrocardiographs on every ward.
  • Ultrasound imagingObstetric and general scans, same-day reports.
  • Fully automated haematology analyserComplete blood counts in minutes.
  • Semi-automated chemistry analyserKidney, liver and electrolyte panels.
  • Laboratory microscopeMicrobiology & malaria diagnostics.
  • Water bath & lab computerReliable sample incubation and result tracking.
  • Urinalysis machineRapid urine chemistry & microscopy.
  • Roller mixerGentle, consistent mixing for blood samples.
  • Industrial washing machineHigh-temperature linen disinfection.
  • High-dependency patient monitorsContinuous observation on every ward.
  • Two fully equipped ambulancesDispatch across Greater Masaka, 24/7.

    Privacy & patient rights

    Your information is safe with us.

    What we collect

    Appointment requests sent through this website are used only to arrange your visit and to contact you about it. We never sell, rent or share patient information with third parties for marketing.

    Medical confidentiality

    All clinical records at Bulamu Hospital are confidential. Information is released only to the patient, a nominated next of kin, or where the law requires it.

    Your rights as a patient

    You have the right to clear explanations in a language you understand, a second opinion, an itemised bill, and to raise a compliment or complaint at the front desk or through care@bulamuhospital.co.ug.
