About the hospital

A hospital for Greater Masaka.

Bulamu Hospital began as a small outpatient clinic serving traders, farmers and boda riders around Masaka's busy town centre. Today it is a forty-five-bed private general hospital — but the name still says everything.

18years of care

Our story

Bulamu is Luganda for life.

Our doctors, nurses and midwives look after more than 26,000 patients a year, from routine malaria treatment to planned surgery and safe deliveries. Positioned on the Kampala–Mbarara highway, we also carry a responsibility few hospitals share: being the closest door for travellers and our neighbours when the worst happens on the road.

We are licensed by the Uganda Medical & Dental Practitioners Council, accredited with all major Ugandan insurers, and run continuous medical education for our team so the standard of care rises every year.

    Privacy & patient rights

    Your information is safe with us.

    What we collect

    Appointment requests sent through this website are used only to arrange your visit and to contact you about it. We never sell, rent or share patient information with third parties for marketing.

    Medical confidentiality

    All clinical records at Bulamu Hospital are confidential. Information is released only to the patient, a nominated next of kin, or where the law requires it.

    Your rights as a patient

    You have the right to clear explanations in a language you understand, a second opinion, an itemised bill, and to raise a compliment or complaint at the front desk or through care@bulamuhospital.co.ug.
