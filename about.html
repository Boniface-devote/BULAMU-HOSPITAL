About Bulamu Medical Clinic

Your 24/7 clinic in the heart of Masaka.

Bulamu Medical Clinic has looked after the people of Masaka from Plot 18 Edward Avenue for years. Bulamu means life — and that is exactly what our team protects, every hour of every day.

Our story

Bulamu is Luganda for life.

Bulamu Medical Clinic is a private general practice on Plot 18 Edward Avenue in the heart of Masaka City, open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. From a routine blood pressure check to an emergency Caesarean section at 3 AM, our doctors, nurses, midwives and lab team are on site — and on call.

We run a fully stocked pharmacy, an in-house laboratory with ECG, ultrasound and digital X-ray, a modern dental suite, a fully equipped operating theatre and an ambulance on standby. We also partner with every major Ugandan insurer so the bill is the least of your worries.

What we do

Everything you need, under one roof.

From a walk-in malaria test to a planned C-section, our clinic is set up to handle the full range of conditions our community faces — without sending you across town.

  • Medical servicesWalk-in and booked consultations, chronic disease management, and general practice.
  • LaboratoryHaematology, chemistry, microbiology and same-day results.
  • ECG & Ultrasound (Scan)Cardiac tracings and obstetric & general ultrasound on site.
  • X-RayDigital X-ray for chest, bones and accident cases.
  • DentalCheck-ups, scaling, fillings, extractions and dentures.
  • TheatreModern operating theatre for elective and emergency surgery.
  • PharmacyFully stocked, open 24 hours — and stockists of NHIS medicines.
  • Gynaecology & ObstetricsAntenatal, postnatal, deliveries and gynaecology clinics.
  • Antenatal servicesFull pregnancy follow-up, with ultrasound on site.
  • ImmunisationWednesday morning baby immunisation clinic (EPI schedule).
  • Ambulance — 24/7Two ambulances on standby for Masaka and the surrounding districts.
  • AdmissionsShort-stay observation and inpatient beds for medical and surgical cases.
  • School healthMedical care and accounts for schools — including term-time check-ups.
  • Driving permit check-upsOfficial medical examinations for new and renewing driving permits.
  • School fitness formsMedical fitness certificates required by schools.
  • Business medical accountsCorporate accounts for staff medical care — speak to our front office.

    Privacy & patient rights

    Your information is safe with us.

    What we collect

    Appointment requests sent through this website are used only to arrange your visit and to contact you about it. We never sell, rent or share patient information with third parties for marketing.

    Medical confidentiality

    All clinical records at Bulamu Hospital are confidential. Information is released only to the patient, a nominated next of kin, or where the law requires it.

    Your rights as a patient

    You have the right to clear explanations in a language you understand, a second opinion, an itemised bill, and to raise a compliment or complaint at the front desk or through care@bulamuhospital.co.ug.
